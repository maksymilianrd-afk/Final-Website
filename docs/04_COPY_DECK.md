## SCENE 10 — FAQ
**H2:** Quiet answers.

**Will it fit my desk?**
Any edge between 20 and 75 mm thick — solid wood, laminate, marble, metal. Not glass: the clamp grips hard, and glass doesn't like being gripped.

**How much weight does it hold?**
Tested to 22 kg. The average cat is 4–5 kg. Even a very committed Maine Coon has a comfortable margin.

**Will it scratch my desk?**
No. Both jaws are felt-padded, and the knob is hand-tightened — you control the pressure. No marks, no residue.

**What if my cat ignores it?**
Give it a day, and try a pinch of catnip in the basket — most cats claim it within 24 hours. If yours holds out for 30 days, send it back. Full refund, no questions, no guilt.

**Can I wash it?**
Yes. The plush cover unzips off the frame and machine-washes on a gentle cycle. Air-dry, fluff, zip back on.

**Is there any assembly?**
About three minutes, no tools: slip the cover over the frame, zip it closed, clamp it to your desk. Everything you need is in the box.

**Can I fold it out of the way?**
Yes — lift the basket and press the side button, and it folds flat against the desk edge. One click down, one lift back up. The clamp stays put.

**Does it work on standing desks?**
Yes — just give the knob a check-turn after big height changes. The clamp doesn't mind the ride.

**Shipping?**
Dispatched within 48 hours, tracked, free over $60. 30-day returns from the day it arrives.
